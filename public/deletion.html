Account & Data Deletion Policy

Effective Date: September 30, 2026 | Last Updated: September 30, 2026

This policy outlines the procedure for requesting the deletion of your user account and associated personal data from iAquila (Android package and iOS bundle com.alabian.aquila) and the web situation room platform at iaquila.com.ng.

App Store Compliance Notice: In compliance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion Policy and Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Section 5.1.1(v)), iAquila provides both in-app and web-based pathways for account and personal data deletion without requiring you to retain or reinstall the application.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

You can request complete deletion of your account and personal data through either of the following methods:

AMethod 1: In-App Deletion (Mobile Application)
  1. Open the iAquila application on your mobile device.
  2. Navigate to the Profile tab (bottom right of the navigation bar).
  3. Scroll down to Account Security & Data Management.
  4. Tap Request Account Deletion.
  5. Confirm your selection when prompted. A confirmation email and security OTP will be dispatched to your registered address to verify identity before executing the purge.
BMethod 2: Web / Email Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, send an email from your registered account address directly to:

Email: privacy@iaquila.com.ng
Subject Line: Account and Data Deletion Request - [Your Full Name or Agent ID]

Please include your:

  • Full Name
  • Registered Email Address
  • Accredited Mission / Organization Name
  • Mobile Phone Number associated with the account

2. What Data Is Permanently Deleted

Upon verification and execution of the deletion request, the following information is permanently purged from our active operational databases:

  • Your personal profile credentials (name, email address, telephone number, login password hashes).
  • Device identifiers, push notification tokens, and hardware fingerprint logs.
  • Secure stored credentials and encrypted local authentication caches.
  • Personal field assignments linking your identity to specific polling stations.

3. Data Retention & Statutory Exceptions

In accordance with Nigerian electoral observation guidelines, statutory legal requirements, and NDPA 2023 provisions regarding public interest archiving:

  • Electoral Collation Data & Incident Records: Tallies submitted from polling stations (Form EC8A vote returns) and documented electoral incident reports are detached from your personal identity and retained in an anonymized, aggregated format as part of the public election audit trail.
  • Legal Hold & Tribunal Records: If any election result or incident report submitted from a polling unit is currently subject to an active petition before the Election Petitions Tribunal or Court of Appeal, cryptographic submission evidence will be preserved strictly until the conclusion of judicial proceedings.

4. Deletion Timeframe

All verified account deletion requests are processed and executed within thirty (30) calendar days of verification. You will receive a final confirmation notification via email once all identifiable personal data has been completely erased.
