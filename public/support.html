Frequently Asked Questions (Field Operations)

How do offline draft results work in areas with poor network coverage?
iAquila features an autonomous cryptographic offline queue. When cellular signal is weak or unavailable at your Polling Unit, submit your counts as usual. The app saves the submission as an encrypted local draft (tagged with your precise GPS coordinates). As soon as your device reconnects to a 3G/4G/5G or Wi-Fi network, open the Drafts Queue and tap Transmit All to upload them directly to the Situation Room.
Why does iAquila require high-accuracy GPS location?
Location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) are used exclusively at the exact moment of result submission or incident reporting. This stamps your Form EC8A submission with cryptographic geographic coordinates to prove that results were recorded directly inside the physically gazetted Polling Unit, preventing unauthorized remote tampering or coordinate spoofing.
Why can't I upload pre-existing photos or videos from my device gallery?
To maintain strict evidentiary integrity for accredited observer missions and election tribunals, iAquila enforces live, in-app media capture. Pre-recorded gallery uploads are intentionally disabled to guarantee that all photos, audio recordings, and video clips were recorded live at the polling station.
What are the different roles (Field Agent, Polling Unit Agent, Election Officer)?
  • Polling Unit Agent: Dedicated to a single assigned Polling Unit for recording accredited voters, ballot returns, and Form EC8A figures.
  • Field Agent: Supervisors managing a cluster of multiple Polling Units within a Ward or LGA.
  • Election Officer / Situation Room Analyst: Supervisory read-and-audit access over the national collation room, incident triage queue, and AI projection engine.
How do I reset my credentials or change my assigned Polling Unit?
Credentials and Polling Unit assignments are managed by your accredited observer organization's administrator. Contact your mission lead or email support@iaquila.com.ng with your Observer Accreditation ID and registered email.
How do I request deletion of my account and personal data?
You can request account deletion directly in the mobile app by visiting Profile → Request Account Deletion, or by reviewing the step-by-step instructions on our Account & Data Deletion Policy page.
