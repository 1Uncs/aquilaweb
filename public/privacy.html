Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 30, 2026 | Last Updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how iAquila and Alabian Solutions Limited ("we", "us", or "our") collect, use, process, and protect your information across our mobile application (iAquila, Android package and iOS bundle com.alabian.aquila) and our web command and situation room platform accessible via iaquila.com.ng and its authorized subdomains.

NDPA & NDPR Compliance: This policy is formulated in full accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), and applicable election observation transparency standards.

1. Scope & Purpose of the Application

iAquila is an independent, accredited election monitoring and parallel collation software suite designed for accredited observer missions, field agents, polling unit agents, and election officers in the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Its primary operational objectives are verifying ballot integrity, collating field results, recording election day incidents, and maintaining verifiable election audit trails.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information strictly required to carry out election monitoring and verify authenticity:

  • Account & Identity Credentials: User name, accredited observer badge/ID number, official email address, phone number, designated operational role (Field Agent, Polling Unit Agent, Election Officer, or Situation Room Analyst), and organization affiliation.
  • Precise Geolocation Data: High-accuracy GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, horizontal accuracy) collected when you submit an election result or report an incident. We do not track continuous background location; location is only sampled upon explicit result/incident transmission to cryptographically geotag field evidence and prevent location spoofing.
  • Audio and Video Recordings: Live voice notes and video captures initiated by you via the application to substantiate real-time electoral incident reports (e.g., ballot disruption, PU delay, logistics failure). Arbitrary gallery scraping is disabled to guarantee authentic live field capture.
  • Election Data & Collation Submissions: Polling Unit (PU) codes, Ward, Local Government Area (LGA), State, registered voter counts, accredited voter counts, rejected ballots, and party vote distributions.
  • Technical & Device Diagnostics: Device model, operating system version, network connectivity status, crash telemetry, and secure cryptographic tokens stored on-device to enable robust offline operation in rural or low-connectivity areas.

3. Device Permissions Requested

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: To verify that results and incidents originate from the physically designated polling unit.
  • RECORD_AUDIO: To record brief audio descriptions of field incidents.
  • CAMERA: To capture live photo and video evidence of Form EC8A and polling station events.
  • SECURE_STORE: To safeguard offline draft collation data and session authentication tokens.

4. How We Use and Process Your Data

We process collected data under the legitimate public interest of democratic electoral observation:

  • Authenticating accredited observers and validating access permissions.
  • Parallel collation, duplicate detection, and mathematical verification of election tallies.
  • Situational awareness and rapid dispatch for election monitors during critical incidents.
  • Statistical projection modeling and anomaly detection.

5. Third-Party Sharing & Data Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or commercialize your personal information. Data collected is strictly confidential and shared only under authorized mandates:

  • Accredited Situation Room HQ: Verified tallies and incident logs are transmitted to your accredited observer mission's situation room directors.
  • Official Observer Reports: Aggregate, anonymized statistical results and documented election incidents may be included in public observer mission post-election reports. Personal identifying agent credentials are never published in public reports.
  • Legal Mandates: Where required by Nigerian law, lawful subpoenas, or the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) dispute tribunals for evidentiary verification.

6. Data Storage, Security & Offline Cache

Given the mission-critical nature of election monitoring, data is secured using end-to-end encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and AES-256 encryption at rest. Field drafts are encrypted in the device’s hardware-backed keystore until securely transmitted to the central situation room.

7. Data Retention & Rights

Personal credentials are kept for the active duration of the observer mission and election cycle. Audit logs of election returns are preserved in accordance with statutory electoral challenge periods. Under the NDPA 2023, you have the right to request access to your personal data, rectify inaccuracies, or request account and data deletion as described in our Account & Data Deletion Policy.
